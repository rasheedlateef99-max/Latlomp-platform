Online Payments — Coming Soon

LatLomp is building a secure online payment feature that will allow parents and students to pay fees directly through the platform — without leaving the portal.

When it launches, it will plug directly into your school's existing fee structure, assignment, and Finance verification workflow — the same architecture you use today, with no duplicate payment engines and no surprise service fees.

Current workflow: School configures payment accounts → Parent or student selects account → Makes external bank transfer → Submits claim → Finance verifies → Official receipt issued.
