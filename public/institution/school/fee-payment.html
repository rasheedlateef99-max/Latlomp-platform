This section is for recording payments that have already been physically received by the school (cash, bank transfer, cheque confirmed in the school's actual bank account).
Staff-recorded payments are immediately treated as confirmed verified transactions.
This is different from a payment claim submitted by a parent or student. Payer-submitted claims will be available as a separate workflow in a future update. Only authorised school financial staff should use this section.
